Brightday ("the app") is made by [YOUR NAME OR COMPANY] ("we"). This policy
explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## The short version

- Your plans live on your phone. We don't have accounts, servers that store
  your tasks, ads, or analytics.
- If you tap **Break it down** with AI turned on, the task name and notes for
  that one task are sent to our AI service to suggest steps, and are not
  stored by us.

## Information stored on your device

Tasks, steps, notes, times, reminders and settings (including the optional
first name you enter) are stored only in the app's private storage on your
device. Android may include this data in your device's own backup to your
Google account, under your Google account settings. You can delete everything
at any time in Settings → Delete all my data, or by uninstalling the app.

## AI task breakdown

When AI breakdown is on and you tap **Break it down**, the app sends the
task's name and notes over an encrypted connection to our breakdown service
(hosted on Cloudflare), which forwards them to Anthropic's Claude API to
generate steps. We do not store this text or link it to you. Anthropic
processes it under its commercial terms and does not use API data to train
its models; see anthropic.com/legal for details. Cloudflare may keep standard
short-lived request logs (such as IP address) for security.

You can turn AI breakdown off in Settings. The app then uses built-in
suggestions and sends nothing.

## Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No reminder data leaves the
phone.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
