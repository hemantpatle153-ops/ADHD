## Short description (max 80)

See your day, break big tasks into tiny steps, and focus with a calm buddy.

## Full description (max 4000)

Brightday is a visual day planner made for ADHD brains, and for anyone who
loses track of time or freezes in front of a big task.

SEE YOUR WHOLE DAY
Your plan is a colourful timeline. Every block is as tall as it is long, and a
"now" line shows exactly where you are, so time stops being invisible. A
"Now / Next up" card tells you how many minutes are left or how soon the next
thing starts.

BIG TASKS, TINY STEPS
Stuck on "clean the kitchen" or "write the essay"? Tap Break it down and get
small, concrete steps. The first one is always easy, because starting is the
hardest part.

FOCUS WITH A BUDDY
Focus mode gives you a visual timer that shrinks as time passes, your current
step front and centre, and a calm buddy who checks in every few minutes, like
working next to a friend. Pause, add five minutes, or finish early.

GENTLE, NOT NAGGING
Soft reminders before things start. Unfinished tasks roll forward with one
tap, with no red overdue lists and no guilt.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. Your plans stay on your phone. No ads, no tracking.

Also: light and dark themes, reduce-motion mode, haptic feedback you can turn
off, and screen reader support.

Brightday is a planning tool. It is not a medical device and does not
diagnose or treat ADHD.
